Welcome. This site publishes my notes, organized with the [PARA method](https://fortelabs.com/blog/para/) by Tiago Forte.

## Sections

- [[1 - Projects/Example Project|Projects]]: efforts with a goal and a deadline
- [[2 - Areas/Example Area|Areas]]: ongoing responsibilities with a standard to maintain
- [[3 - Resources/Example Resource|Resources]]: topics and reference material I want to keep
- [[4 - Archive/Example Archived Note|Archive]]: inactive items from the other three

The `Inbox` is where new notes land first. It is private and never published.
